Welcome to Luna's Island

Luna looks after her pups Kiko and Bruno, her cats and bunnies, a garden, and a little seaside market. Lady Moon crafts, and Solon keeps the island running. They live their own day, and you can join in.

  • Watch with Auto cam, or drag to look around.
  • Earn coins as Luna harvests, fishes and sells at the Market.
  • Island life: Luna, Lady Moon and Solon share the chores. Solon feeds the pups, herds them at dusk and sweeps when the others are busy.
  • Decorate both islands, chop trees for wood, and build.
  • Play in Controller mode with a thumb stick. Press M to cycle Luna, Lady Moon and Solon.
